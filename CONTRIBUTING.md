# Contributing to Ai-engineering-roadmap

Thank you for your interest in contributing to this AI Engineering Roadmap journey!

## How You Can Help
- **Resource Suggestions:** Got great free/open AI/ML resources? Add them as Issues or Pull Requests.
- **Correction/Improvements:** See a typo, broken link, or doc fix? PRs welcome!
- **Learning Tips:** If you’re on the same path and found something helpful, share your experience in Discussions or via Issue.
- **Phase Progress:** Feel free to fork and adapt this roadmap for your own journey. Share your phase logs and inspiration.
- **Project Ideas:** Have ideas for projects that fit this roadmap? Suggest via Issue or PR (document your sample repo or code!).

## How to Contribute
1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a branch for your feature, resource, or fix.
3. Make your changes.
4. Open a Pull Request, describing your input or fix.

## Code of Conduct
Please keep all contributions respectful and constructive. This is a safe, supportive place for learning and growing together!

---

For larger suggestions, open an Issue first to discuss.

Thanks for helping advance the open AI learning community! 🙌
